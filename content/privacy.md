## In short

This is a personal academic website. It shows no advertising, sells nothing, profiles nobody, and shares nothing with third parties for marketing. The only measurement tool is Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID `G-R5FJKDQD1M`), which I use to see which research pages and blog posts are read most — and it only collects data if you allow it.

## Your first visit

On your first visit a small banner asks whether anonymous usage statistics may be collected. **Until you choose, nothing is stored**: no analytics cookie is set and no statistics are recorded. If you click **Decline**, it stays that way. If you click **Allow**, the cookies described below are set and Google Analytics starts counting your visits. Your choice is remembered in your browser, so you are asked only once.

## If you allow: what is collected

When you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 receives:

- which pages you open and roughly how long you read them;
- your approximate location (country and city, derived from your IP address);
- your browser, operating system, screen size and language;
- how you arrived at the site (for example a search engine or a link);
- the date and time of your visit.

The cookies Google Analytics sets are:

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Stores an anonymous client ID that distinguishes your browser from others | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` (the one belonging to this site's measurement ID `G-R5FJKDQD1M`) | Persists session state, so consecutive page views count as one visit | 2 years |

These are first-party cookies: they belong to this site and can only be read here. Browsers may shorten the lifetime automatically. The reports I see are aggregated — how often a page was read and from roughly where, never who read it.

## If you decline

No analytics cookies are written and no identifiers are stored. Google's "Consent Mode" still sends cookieless signals so that traffic volumes can be estimated; according to [Google's documentation](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/consent) these signals contain no persistent identifiers and cannot identify you.

## Where the data goes

Google Analytics is operated by Google. Data is processed and stored on Google's servers outside India (typically the United States) under Google's global privacy policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Changing your mind

Your choice is kept in your browser's local storage. To change it, clear the site data for ankitdeshmukh.com (in Chrome: padlock icon → *Site settings* → *Delete data*; in Firefox: *Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Exceptions*), reload the page, and the banner will ask you again.
